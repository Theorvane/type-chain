# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and focusing on the overall community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@theorvane.tech**. Maintainers may remove or reject conduct and contributions that violate these standards. Reports are reviewed fairly and confidentially.
